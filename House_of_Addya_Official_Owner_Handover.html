3 Owner Daily Verification & Dispatch Protocol

When a customer places an order on House of Addya, follow this 4-step workflow to verify payment and dispatch:

01
Login to Admin CMS (/admin) — Open the Owner Dashboard on phone or laptop. Enter PIN (1122). Click Orders & Shipping.
02
Check Customer Payment UTR Reference — Each UPI or Bank Transfer order clearly highlights the customer's 12-digit reference number (e.g. Payment Ref/UTR: 428901238491) right under the address.
03
Cross-Verify in Bank App (10 Seconds) — Open Punjab National Bank NetBanking or HDFC/PhonePe business notifications to confirm credit matching order total and customer UTR.
04
Assign Shiprocket AWB & Update Status — Pack the garment in branded House of Addya packaging. Paste the Shiprocket AWB tracking number (e.g. SR-884761452) into the field and switch status to Shipped.

To build high trust with Gen-Z shoppers, the platform highlights the 7-day doorstep exchange guarantee. Customers can initiate exchanges via the WhatsApp concierge or support email (hello@houseofaddya.com).
